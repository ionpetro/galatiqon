# Galatiqon: print it

Three parts, no supports, no screws, no inserts. Generated by `galatiqon_print.py`
(manifold3d + trimesh; run it with any venv that has `manifold3d trimesh numpy matplotlib`).
The STLs land in `stl/` here and in `web/public/models/`, where the site's guide links to them.

| Part | File | Print orientation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body (back shell) | `galatiqon-body.stl` | as exported: flat back on the bed | Vertical walls, 2 mm 45° chamfer at the back edge, Pi standoffs and power-bank rails inside, oval button opening in the top wall |
| Face cap | `galatiqon-face.stl` | as exported: face on the bed | Rounded front; the part of the round-over steeper than 45° is a 45° chamfer so nothing needs support. Screen window, two eye slots, grille, mic hole and the G recess are through-cuts / a 0.8 mm pocket |
| Button cap | `galatiqon-button.stl` | as exported: lip plate on the bed | Drops into the top opening from inside; the 16 mm switch pushes it up |

## Settings (PETG or PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle)

- 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 15–25 % infill. The shell is 2.4 mm thick.
- No supports. Brim optional for the face cap (its first layer is the chamfer ring, about 90 × 80 mm of contact).
- The G recess and the screen window are on the bed face: use a smooth PEI sheet for a clean front.
- Size: 112 × 104 × 58 mm assembled. Body 34 mm deep, face cap 27 mm, button cap 49 × 31 × 4.4 mm.
- Plastic: about 100 cm³ solid; around 55 g for the pair at 20 % infill. Roughly 5 h for the body, 4 h for the face at normal speeds.

## Fit

- The face cap has a 1.6 mm tongue that slides inside the body wall with 0.25 mm clearance. It is a
  friction fit: if it is loose on your printer, a strip of thin double-sided tape on the tongue or a
  dab of hot glue holds it; if it is tight, scale the face cap 99.8 % in X and Y.
- Pi mounts on four standoffs for M2.5 screws (self-tapping into 2.4 mm holes, or heat-set inserts
  if you have them). The board stands upright behind the face, parts facing the screen.
- A slim power bank (up to 92 × 60 × 13 mm) lies on the back wall between the two rails, under the Pi.

## What goes where (looking at the face)

- Top: the oval button (16 mm momentary switch behind it, GPIO17 to GND).
- Top right: two slanted slots. Left one over the USB microphone, right one over an LED ring if you add one.
- Top left: the G mark, a 0.8 mm recess. Paint-fill it white, or print the face with a filament swap at layer 4.
- Lower middle: 66 × 17 mm pill window for a 1.9" 170 × 320 bar display.
- Bottom: seven 2.2 mm grille holes over the 40 mm speaker, and a 1.6 mm pinhole for a backup mic.
